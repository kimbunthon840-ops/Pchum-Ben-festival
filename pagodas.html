The Tradition of Visiting 7 Pagodas

On Pchum Thom (Day 15), many Cambodians make it their mission to visit and offer food at seven different pagodas in a single day. Buddhist folklore tells that wandering ancestral spirits check each temple searching for their family. If a family visits seven pagodas, it is guaranteed they will cross paths with their ancestors and bestow profound merit.
