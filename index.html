RITUAL OF COMPASSION

Bos Bay Ben (Throwing Rice Balls)

Devotees circumambulate the temple 3 times before sunrise, tossing sanctified sesame rice balls under the morning stars to nourish wandering ancestral spirits (Preta).

☸️ Sangha Blessing
MERIT TRANSFER

Bangskol & Merit Transfer

Buddhist monks chant sacred Pali verses over family ancestral urns and white cloth, transmuting worldly good deeds directly into spiritual relief for 7 generations.

Explore Bangskol →
⛰️ Karma Cleansing
MOUNT MERU SYMBOLISM

Phnum Khsach (Sand Mountain)

Devotees sculpt five decorative sand stupas in pagoda courtyards representing Mount Meru and the five Buddhas, dissolving as many sins as there are grains of sand.

View Symbolism →
🍌 Sweet Delicacy
SYMBOL OF FERTILITY

Num Ansom Chek

Cylindrical sticky rice cake packed with sweet baby bananas, bound tightly with split bamboo and boiled over wood fires for 8 to 10 hours.

View Recipe & Story →
🔺 Pyramid Shape
COSMIC FEMININE DUALITY

Num Kom (Pyramid Cake)

Pyramidal rice dumplings stuffed with roasted grated coconut and caramelized palm sugar, representing goddess Uma and the sacred earth.

KRATIE & BATTAMBANG SPECIALTY

Kralan in Bamboo

Fragrant sticky rice, black beans, coconut cream, and a pinch of salt packed inside young bamboo stalks and roasted over glowing charcoal.
