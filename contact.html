COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can non-Buddhists participate in Bos Bay Ben?

Yes! Cambodian pagodas welcome respectful visitors of all faiths. Simply dress in modest white clothing, maintain quiet mindfulness, and follow the circumambulation.

What should I bring when visiting a pagoda?

Traditional offerings include Num Ansom cakes, fresh lotus blossoms, candles, incense sticks, and a monetary donation placed inside an envelope for temple maintenance.

How much should I donate to monks or the pagoda?

There is no mandatory amount; donations depend on your heart (Citta) and capacity. Small envelopes of 2,000 to 10,000 Riels (or $1 to $5 USD) are common during general alms, or larger donations for pagoda building projects.
