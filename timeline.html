TEMPLE RHYTHM

Typical Daily Schedule During Kann Ben

Every day of Kann Ben follows an ancient monastic tempo designed to dedicate maximum spiritual blessings.

🌙
04:00 AM

Bos Bay Ben

Circumambulating the Vihear 3 times and tossing sesame rice balls under candle light.

☀️
07:00 AM

Tak Bat (Alms)

Placing fresh rice, curry, and Num Ansom cakes into the monks' sacred alms bowls.

📜
01:00 PM

Dhamma Preaching

Monks expound Buddhist ethics, the law of Karma, and teachings on filial gratitude.

🕯️
06:00 PM

Evening Paritta

Chanting protective verses and meditation as night settles over the temple courtyard.
