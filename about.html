SPIRITUAL FOUNDATION

What is Pchum Ben?

Understanding Cambodia's most sacred period of compassion, karma, and generational continuity.

LITERAL MEANING

‘Pchum’ to Gather, ‘Ben’ a Ball of Rice

In the Khmer language, ‘Pchum’ (ភ្ជុំ) means to gather or assemble, while ‘Ben’ (បិណ្ឌ), derived from the Pali term ‘Pinda’, signifies a molded ball of rice or food offering. Together, Pchum Ben symbolizes both the gathering of living kin and the assembly of ancestral spirits receiving sacred offerings.

Celebrated during the 10th month of the Khmer lunar calendar (Bhadrapada), it corresponds with the rainy season retreat (Vassa) when monks reside in their pagodas.

👻
THE REALM OF PETAS

The Hungry Ghosts (Preta)

In Buddhist cosmology, beings with heavy karmic burdens from greed, cruelty, or stinginess are reborn as Preta (hungry ghosts)—spirits enduring perpetual thirst and hunger.

ANCESTRAL LINEAGE

Seven Generations of Relatives

Cambodians dedicate merit across seven genealogical tiers—4 ascending generations and 3 descending generations—ensuring no departed soul is left forgotten.

4 Ascending Ancestors ⬆️ Past
4. លួត (G-G-Grandparents) 3. ជីទួត (G-Grandparents) 2. ជីដូនជីតា (Grandparents) 1. ឪពុកម្តាយ (Parents)
⭐ Living Self (The Sacred Anchor)
3 Descending Lineages ⬇️ Future
1. កូន (Children) 2. ចៅ (Grandchildren) 3. ចៅទួត (Great-Grandchildren)
✨ Universal Coverage: Liberating all related lineages from samsaric suffering.

GATE OF MERCIES 15-DAY LUNAR WINDOW

Why Are the Spirits Released During This Period?

According to ancient Khmer tradition, King Yama, the Lord of the Underworld, temporarily opens the gates of the spirit realm during the dark waning moon of Bhadrapada. Deceased relatives are permitted to wander the earth seeking their living descendants at pagodas.

🌒
Waning Moon of Bhadrapada
Veil between worlds is at its thinnest
⚖️
King Yama's Royal Reprieve
15 days of suspended karmic suffering
