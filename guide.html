CULTURAL RESPECT

Visitor Etiquette & Tips

Essential customs, dress codes, and practical guidelines for locals and international visitors.

✅

What to Do (Respectful Actions)

  • ✓
    Wear Modest White Attire
    Shoulders and knees must be covered. White tops symbolize purity and peaceful mourning.
  • ✓
    Remove Shoes & Hats
    Take off footwear and headwear before entering any temple hall (Preah Vihear).
  • ✓
    Perform the Sampeah Greeting
    Press your palms together in front of your chest to greet elders and monks respectfully.
  • ✓
    Tuck Feet When Sitting
    Sit with your legs folded beneath or to the side. Never point the soles of your feet towards monks or Buddha statues.
❌

What to Avoid (Taboos)

  • ✗
    Do Not Touch Monks (Especially Women)
    Women should never make physical contact with monks or hand items directly; place offerings on a cloth or tray.
  • ✗
    Avoid Revealing Clothes
    Shorts, mini-skirts, sleeveless tops, and transparent fabrics are strictly prohibited on temple grounds.
  • ✗
    No Loud Talking or Disruptions
    Maintain silence and reverence, particularly during quiet monk sermons and early dawn Bos Bay Ben.
  • ✗
    Do Not Step Over Offerings
    During Bos Bay Ben, walk mindfully around the vihear so you do not step upon rice balls on the earth.
HOLIDAY LOGISTICS

Cambodia Travel Tips During Pchum Ben

🚗 Book Transportation Early

Hundreds of thousands of workers travel from Phnom Penh to their home provinces. Bus, van, and train tickets sell out days in advance.

🏪 Quiet Phnom Penh Streets

During the final 3 public holiday days, Phnom Penh transforms into a tranquil, peaceful city with minimal traffic, as local markets and shops close.

🌅 Wake Up Early at 3:30 AM

To witness Bos Bay Ben, arrive at your chosen pagoda before 4:00 AM. Bring a small flashlight and modest white attire.
